# NEXUS AI — Visual Direction

## Ground-Truth Reference Specification

The supplied cinematic hero and product-page specification is the authoritative reference for this project. The site must preserve its deep-navy atmosphere, live-action fullscreen video treatment, Instrument Serif-led typography, translucent liquid-glass controls, calm editorial spacing, and subtle vertical entrance motion. NEXUS AI replaces the reference brand and content, but does not introduce a competing design system.

## Chosen Design Direction: Quiet Machine Cinema

### Design Movement

**Cinematic editorial minimalism** translated into a software product experience: expansive, moody, and highly restrained, where the interface feels like a controlled layer above a moving world.

### Core Principles

1. **Video carries the atmosphere.** The hero video is the only major visual spectacle; interface surfaces remain transparent and quiet.
2. **Typography creates hierarchy.** Large Instrument Serif statements make product ideas feel considered rather than promotional, while compact sans-serif copy carries technical precision.
3. **Glass is functional, not decorative.** Every translucent surface provides navigation, action, state, or context without overwhelming the background.
4. **Execution is legible.** Product workflows use ordered sequences, status language, and understated linework so autonomy is understandable at a glance.

### Color Philosophy

Deep blue-black anchors the experience and gives the video room to breathe. Soft white communicates clarity, while fog-gray copy, dim borders, and restrained cyan status lights distinguish priority without introducing a separate accent palette. The goal is night-time focus rather than generic "AI futurism."

### Layout Paradigm

The page is a **cinematic descent**: a full-viewport opening sequence drops into a continuous field of deep navy panels, where alternating editorial text compositions and agent-console moments form a linear narrative. Containers are intentionally offset and varied rather than arranged as repeating centered card grids.

### Signature Elements

1. **Liquid-glass capsules** with slim luminous edge treatment for actions and small status controls.
2. **Fine execution rails** — vertical or horizontal hairline paths with numbered steps, status pips, and carefully spaced milestones.
3. **Atmospheric type blocks** — oversized serif statements interrupted by muted phrases, echoing the hero’s typography treatment.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should make NEXUS AI feel composed and inspectable. Controls respond with a brief, precise scale shift; status elements illuminate gently; anchor navigation eases to the next part of the product story. No playful or noisy motion is used.

### Animation

Hero content rises into view in three restrained stages. Section content reveals on scroll with a short upward drift and opacity transition, staggered by 50–80 ms. Execution trails use a subtle traveling indicator only when motion is permitted. All nonessential animation is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Instrument Serif** is used for hero, section statements, and key closers at normal weight with tight tracking and deliberate line breaks. **DM Sans** is used for navigation, body copy, labels, code-adjacent UI, and status descriptions. Serif headlines are expansive; technical UI remains compact and calm.

### Brand Essence

**NEXUS AI is a locally installed autonomous agent for people who want to delegate real digital work without losing sight of how it is done.**

Personality: **composed, capable, deliberate.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are direct, quiet, and declarative; CTAs describe a meaningful next action. Microcopy avoids inflated claims and emphasizes observable execution.

Example headline: “Don’t just ask AI. Give it a job.”

Example CTA: “Watch a task take shape.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is a custom spaced NEXUS AI name in restrained uppercase sans-serif, paired with an abstract **split-nexus mark**: two interlocking orbital strokes that create a focused aperture at their meeting point. The symbol is graphic-only, white with a subtle cyan edge, and works at favicon scale.

### Signature Brand Color

**Nexus Signal — #8AE8FF**: a pale electric cyan used only for live execution states, active pips, and focused controls.

## Style Decisions

- Maintain the supplied **fullscreen background video** as the exclusive visual source in the hero; do not add gradients, blobs, or artificial image overlays.
- Adapt the referenced full-page structure into a cohesive NEXUS AI story: meet the agent, understand how it works, inspect execution, understand control, install it, and begin.
- Do not show unverified software versions, fake release routes, fictitious testimonials, invented customer claims, or unsupported privacy/security assertions.
- The NEXUS AI identity uses a custom-spaced uppercase wordmark paired with the split-nexus aperture mark in the header, footer, and closing action; it must never collapse into a default small app label.
- Product lists should read as inspectable execution rails, traces, numbered states, or agent-console records rather than ordinary SaaS card grids.
- Nexus Signal is a state color: it denotes live work, focused controls, verified outcomes, and terminal execution states.
- The supplied video remains the hero’s sole visual depth source, in accordance with the original reference specification; no artificial overlay or substitute imagery will be added to it.
- The complete brand name is **NEXUS AI**. The custom-spaced uppercase wordmark and white/cyan split-nexus aperture mark appear consistently in navigation, the hero, footer, and closing moment.
- Purple or magenta asset coloration is not part of the approved system. The aperture mark is treated in white with a restrained Nexus Signal cyan edge or glow.
- Fine execution rails, numbered states, pips, and traces unify the whole page into one observable workflow rather than isolated product panels.
- Every primary brand moment uses **NEXUS AI**. “NEXUS CLI” is reserved only for the separately named terminal interface.
- Fullscreen cinematic video is the hero depth source across primary entry pages; navigation, typography, and terminal surfaces remain restrained layers above it.
- The page rhythm alternates split editorial sections with full-width execution traces and oversized serif statements, avoiding a repeated card-grid cadence.
- Secondary pages use a page-specific cinematic execution artifact—such as a release feed, workflow map, or approval state—rather than reusing a generic orbit-and-card hero composition.
- Low-emphasis reference material may use panels, but primary lists default to execution rails, source feeds, terminal records, or status maps.
- Liquid-glass actions and consoles maintain translucent depth, luminous hairlines, and restrained cyan state signals without introducing new colors, gradients, or decorative noise.
